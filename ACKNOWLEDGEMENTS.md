# Acknowledgements

Chatinabox is a small bridge built on excellent, much larger systems.

## Foundations

- [OpenAI Codex](https://github.com/openai/codex) is the intelligence and
  terminal experience Chatinabox carries into Telegram.
- [Telegram Bot API](https://core.telegram.org/bots/api) provides the remote
  interface.
- [tmux](https://github.com/tmux/tmux) makes sessions durable, discoverable,
  and attachable.
- [Node.js](https://nodejs.org/) and [SQLite](https://www.sqlite.org/) power
  the bridge and its local state.
- [Chromium](https://www.chromium.org/) and
  [ImageMagick](https://imagemagick.org/) help turn terminal state into clear,
  mobile-friendly screen views.
- [markdown-it](https://github.com/markdown-it/markdown-it) and
  [rebrowser-puppeteer-core](https://github.com/rebrowser/rebrowser-puppeteer-core)
  support Telegram-native formatting and terminal rendering.

Thank you to the people who build and maintain them.

The repository's spare visual language takes cues from
[Frontier](https://github.com/somewhereafter/frontier), a sibling project. It
is a design influence, not a code dependency.

The name grew from **catinabox**, the original bot nickname: a small collision
of *chat*, “AI in a box,” and Schrödinger's cat.
